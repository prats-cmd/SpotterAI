Fuel Your Performance

Smart diet, nutrition & supplement guidance to power every rep

🍽️

Diet Basics

  • Eat whole foods — lean protein, complex carbs, healthy fats
  • Time carbs around workouts for energy & recovery
  • Prioritize 7–9 hrs sleep — recovery starts in the kitchen
📊

Macros Guide

Protein 1.6–2.2g/kg Carbs 3–5g/kg Fats 0.8–1g/kg
  • Adjust based on goals — cut, maintain, or bulk
  • Track for 2 weeks, then eat intuitively
💧

Hydration

  • Drink 2.5–3.5 L water daily (more on training days)
  • Add electrolytes during intense or long sessions
  • Check urine color — pale yellow is the goal
💊

Supplements

  • Whey/Plant Protein — hit daily protein targets
  • Creatine Monohydrate — 3–5g daily, well-researched
  • Vitamin D + Omega-3 — support recovery & joints
  • Caffeine — pre-workout boost when needed
⚡

Meal Timing Tips

  • Pre-workout (1–2 hrs): carbs + moderate protein — banana & Greek yogurt, oats & whey
  • Post-workout (within 2 hrs): protein + fast carbs — chicken & rice, protein shake & fruit
  • Rest days: slightly lower carbs, keep protein high for muscle repair
